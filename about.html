About KCC

Kabankalan Catholic College is a Christ-centered learning community committed to academic excellence, faith formation, and service since 1927.

  1. Home
  2. About Us

A Legacy of Faith and Education

Founded in 1927, KCC has served Kabankalan City and surrounding communities through inclusive, values-based education.

  • Academic Excellence: challenging students to think critically and learn with purpose.
  • Faith Formation: strengthening Catholic values, prayer, and personal conviction.
  • Holistic Development: preparing students for leadership, service, and lifelong growth.
